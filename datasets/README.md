# Real Time Speech Captioning: data plan

Status: **Planned**. No datasets or participant records are included.

Scope: Prototype streaming transcription with authorised or synthetic recordings. Before acquiring data, document its source, version, access terms, redistribution rights, permitted uses, provenance, preprocessing and split strategy. Prefer synthetic or appropriately licensed non-sensitive material for initial work. Do not infer permission to redistribute from permission to download.

This project is exploratory research and is not a clinically validated medical device.
No identifiable participant data will be published. Human-participant experiments
require appropriate research ethics approval before recruitment or data collection.
Datasets and trained models may have licensing requirements separate from the MIT
software license. Computational results must not be presented as demonstrated
benefit to hearing-assistance users without appropriate evidence.
