# Real Time Speech Captioning: results policy

Status: **Planned**. There are no experimental results or benchmark figures to report.

Future reports for real-time-speech-captioning must link to a documented experiment, input provenance and exact code/environment revision. Report the chosen measures, uncertainty where appropriate, baseline comparison, failure cases and limitations. Do not imply generalisation beyond the evaluated setting. Store large or restricted artefacts outside Git with reviewed access and licence information.

This project is exploratory research and is not a clinically validated medical device.
No identifiable participant data will be published. Human-participant experiments
require appropriate research ethics approval before recruitment or data collection.
Datasets and trained models may have licensing requirements separate from the MIT
software license. Computational results must not be presented as demonstrated
benefit to hearing-assistance users without appropriate evidence.
